# TMaps

## Community-verified everyday infrastructure, ready for any city

TMaps is a mobile-first web platform that helps residents and visitors find the
everyday services that are often missing, outdated, or hard to verify on general
maps: public restrooms, publicly accessible business restrooms, drinking water,
phone charging, EV charging, fuel stations, and other useful facilities.

The product is already a working installable PWA. It combines a fast map,
location-aware discovery, directions, community reports, amenity feedback, and
moderated submissions in one white-label platform.

### What makes it useful

- multiple infrastructure categories can be viewed together;
- the nearest suitable location can be found from the user's live position;
- users can report closures or incorrect details from the map;
- remote submissions can be reviewed before publication;
- every location can carry practical attributes such as accessibility, price,
  opening conditions, water, soap, paper, and charging availability;
- ordinary, dark, and satellite maps are included;
- there is no app-store barrier: the PWA installs on Android and iPhone;
- the interface and categories can be branded for a city, nonprofit, retailer,
  travel network, or smart-restroom operator.

### Partnership options

**City or state pilot**

Launch a verified public-amenities map for one district or city, connect official
open data, and add a moderated channel for closures and corrections.

**Enterprise white label**

Publish a branded amenity layer for a retailer, fuel network, mobility provider,
or restroom operator and show verified services at every location.

**Health and accessibility program**

Provide urgent-restroom discovery, accessibility details, multilingual UX, and
community verification for patient and public-health organizations.

### Proposed first pilot

A 12-week paid pilot for one city, district, or location network:

1. import and normalize the partner's existing locations;
2. brand the web app and define verification rules;
3. verify a focused launch area;
4. enable moderated community updates;
5. deliver usage, data-quality, and correction metrics;
6. agree on an annual license only after measurable pilot results.

Suggested pilot range: **US$50,000–120,000**, depending on data work,
integrations, moderation, and procurement requirements.

### Current stage

TMaps is a functioning pre-revenue MVP, not a claim of complete or perfectly
current coverage. A commercial deployment would include a licensed production
map provider, partner-approved data sources, privacy and accessibility review,
analytics, service-level terms, and a defined moderation workflow.
