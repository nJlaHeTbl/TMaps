# TMaps: US partner and buyer shortlist

Updated: 24 August 2026. No message or form has been sent.

## Priority 1 — strategic product partners

### Good2Go

- **Why it fits:** Good2Go sells automated restroom access, virtual queuing,
  analytics, and enterprise pilots. TMaps adds a public discovery layer,
  community feedback, broader amenity categories, and international PWA reach.
- **Offer:** a paid integration prototype or white-label discovery experience;
  do not lead with a full IP sale.
- **Route:** official enterprise sales page and phone.
- **Source:** <https://info.bgood2go.com/talk-to-sales>

### Crohn's & Colitis Foundation — We Can't Wait

- **Why it fits:** the Foundation already operates a U.S. restroom finder with
  more than 50,000 locations, business partners, accessibility details, and
  community submissions. Its scale proves the need, while TMaps can offer a
  modern web/PWA layer, multilingual UX, remote-submission review, and additional
  public-infrastructure categories.
- **Offer:** licensed UX/data-quality pilot under their existing brand; never
  imply that their current product should simply be replaced.
- **Route:** Open Restrooms Movement business/municipality form; ask for the
  digital product or partnerships owner.
- **Sources:**
  - <https://www.crohnscolitisfoundation.org/patientsandcaregivers/wecantwait>
  - <https://www.crohnscolitisfoundation.org/openrestrooms/signup-business>

### TOTO USA

- **Why it fits:** TOTO has invested in a restroom-access app and has collaborated
  on connected smart-restroom systems. This is evidence of strategic interest in
  consumer access and facility technology, not proof that TOTO wants to buy
  TMaps.
- **Offer:** sponsor or co-develop a community discovery and feedback layer for
  connected public restrooms, airports, stadiums, retail, and travel facilities.
- **Route:** corporate innovation/business development introduction; public
  relations should only be used to request the correct business contact.
- **Sources:**
  - <https://www.totousa.com/filemanager_uploads/article_press/Notice_of_Investment_GOOD2GO_PR_FINAL.pdf>
  - <https://www.totousa.com/filemanager_uploads/presskits/TOTO_MachineQ_Collaboration_PR_FINAL.pdf>

## Priority 2 — credibility and municipal channels

### American Restroom Association

- **Why it fits:** national advocacy organization with municipal and policy
  connections. It is more likely to validate a pilot or introduce TMaps than to
  pay an enterprise acquisition price.
- **Offer:** a no-obligation demo and a narrowly scoped co-designed city pilot.
- **Route:** `getinfo@americanrestroom.org`, +1 800-247-3864.
- **Source:** <https://americanrestroom.org/>

### New York City public-restroom program

- **Why it fits:** NYC has an official map and active restroom expansion program.
  The City Council's 2025 analysis says operating-status information may be
  outdated and calls for more accurate, near-real-time updates. TMaps already
  has the beginnings of reports, corrections, amenities, and moderation.
- **Offer:** a paid 12-week district pilot that sits beside the official dataset
  and measures closure-report speed and data accuracy. Do not propose replacing
  the citywide platform on the first contact.
- **Route:** identify the responsible agency/product owner and procurement path;
  no cold form should be submitted before confirming the exact recipient.
- **Sources:**
  - <https://council.nyc.gov/data/public-restroom-analysis/>
  - <https://www.nyc.gov/site/mopd/publications/accessiblenyc-2025-report-built.page>

## Negotiation rules

1. Sell a measurable paid pilot first, not the entire codebase.
2. Keep the TMaps IP and offer a nonexclusive annual license.
3. Never promise complete coverage or live accuracy before the partner supplies
   official data and a moderation process exists.
4. Separate setup, annual license, data operations, and support in the proposal.
5. Do not give an exclusive price before knowing territory, data rights, support
   burden, security requirements, and whether the buyer wants the brand or only
   the software.
6. If acquisition is raised, request a written indication of interest and due
   diligence scope before naming a final price.
